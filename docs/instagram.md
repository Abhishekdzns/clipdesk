# Connect Instagram

Instagram has no official upload API for scheduled Reels without Meta app review, so Clipdesk posts through Meta Business Suite in its own browser profile.

1. Switch your Instagram to a Business or Creator account and link it to a Facebook Page.
2. Run `ig_login.bat`. A browser window opens with a profile saved in `ig_profile/`.
3. Sign in to Facebook and Instagram yourself in that window. Clipdesk never types your password.
4. Close it. Posting reuses the saved session.

If the session expires, sign in again the same way. Do not share the `ig_profile/` folder.
